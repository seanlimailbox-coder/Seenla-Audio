# Open Dynamics — Beta 1

**October 18, 2026**

Four macOS audio plugins for shaping transients, controlling dynamics, reducing noise, and managing final output levels.

| Plugin | Version | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open Clipper | 2.4.23 Beta 1 | Shape transients, add density, and control peaks. |
| Open Compressor | 2.10.15 Beta 1 | Control dynamic range and shape attack and sustain. |
| Open Gate | 1.8.37 Beta 1 | Reduce noise and spill, or tighten the space between events. |
| Open Limiter | 7.3.48 Beta 1 | Set final output levels and monitor peaks and loudness. |

## Download and install

Download **`Open-Dynamics-Beta-1-macOS-universal-2026-10-18.pkg`** below. The installer lets you choose any plugins and select **Audio Unit (AU)**, **VST3**, or both for each one.

- **macOS 11 or later**, with Apple Silicon or Intel.
- Requires a host that supports AU or VST3 effects.
- Installs for your current macOS account.
- Quit your audio applications before installation. Reopen and rescan afterwards if needed.

The installer is **unsigned and not notarized by Apple**, so macOS may block installation. This is a beta release; try it in your usual workflow before using it in an important project.
